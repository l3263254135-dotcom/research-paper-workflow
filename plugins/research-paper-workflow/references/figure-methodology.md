# Figure Methodology

This file separates transferable method advice from project evidence.

## BOOK-SUPPORTED

- Figures and tables are part of scientific reasoning and reader communication.
- A figure should have a clear purpose, readable labels, useful ordering, and a caption that supports independent interpretation.
- Editing proceeds from argument and structure to paragraphs, sentences, words, and presentation details.
- Proof and editorial checks must verify numbering, captions, readability, and consistency.

## ARTICLE-SUPPORTED

- Build figures from verified result records and explicit files.
- Keep data, scripts, results, figures, claims, prose, and reviews linked.
- Use a file-based audit trail for figure changes.

## OUR-DESIGN

- Store a reader question, primary claim, panel roles, placement, visual encoding, and manuscript locations in `figure_manifest.yaml`.
- Require figure plan, storyboard, reallocation log, and figure QA output before manuscript reconstruction.
- Treat accessibility and grayscale behavior as review items rather than inferred guarantees.
