# Figure Caption Checklist

- figure number and short descriptive title;
- question answered by the figure;
- panel descriptions and panel order;
- data source, sample unit, denominator, and time window;
- calculation or transformation summary;
- units, scales, masks, symbols, and abbreviations;
- error bars, shaded areas, confidence intervals, or statistical markers;
- color and line encodings;
- bounded interpretation and unresolved limitation;
- no new fact or stronger claim than the linked result record.
