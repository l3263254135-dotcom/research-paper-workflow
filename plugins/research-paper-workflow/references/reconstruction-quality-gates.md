# Reconstruction Quality Gates

## R1 — Story design

- core question and core claim are explicit;
- every major result has a role in the evidence sequence;
- alternative explanations and conclusion boundary are recorded;
- every source block has a disposition;
- figure sequence is consistent with the story spine.

## R2 — Author approval

- `reconstruction_approval: approved` is explicit;
- author decisions and unresolved disagreements are recorded;
- no formal reconstructed manuscript is generated before approval.

## R3 — Rewrite

- Results, Discussion, Introduction, Conclusion, Abstract, Title, Methods, and captions are aligned;
- every rewritten section and paragraph is traceable;
- wording does not strengthen evidence;
- negative and limiting evidence remains visible.

## R4 — Drift check

- no new numeric token or scientific fact is introduced;
- no original material evidence disappears without a disposition;
- figure, caption, abstract, body, and conclusion agree;
- residual blockers are reported as `NOT_READY_FOR_SUBMISSION`.
