# Figure Quality Gates

## Argument

- every figure has one primary reader question and claim;
- every panel has a function and source result;
- figure order supports the evidence sequence;
- main-text and supplementary placement has a reason.

## Visual expression

- labels, units, denominators, sample units, scales, symbols, uncertainty, and statistical annotations are explicit;
- colors, line types, fonts, and panel order remain interpretable in grayscale and at target size;
- axes and annotations do not exaggerate differences;
- captions are self-contained and do not strengthen the claim.

## Consistency

- figure numbers and正文 references agree;
- figure values match `analysis_results.json` and manuscript text;
- negative, null, reverse, and alternative results remain represented;
- stale, duplicate, orphaned, and unreadable figures are dispositioned.
