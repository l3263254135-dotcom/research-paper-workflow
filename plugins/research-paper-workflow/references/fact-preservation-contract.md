# Fact Preservation Contract

The reconstruction mode protects the following invariants:

- numeric values, units, sample sizes, windows, estimates, intervals, tests, and exact p/q values;
- result IDs, claim IDs, figure IDs, table IDs, source files, scripts, and input hashes;
- null, reverse, failed, exploratory, and alternative results;
- stated limitations, uncertainty, citations, and conclusion qualifiers;
- causal, mechanistic, novelty, and generalization boundaries.

Allowed changes are limited to section order, paragraph order, grouping, transitions, sentence structure, wording, caption organization, and visual presentation. Any changed fact or stronger claim must be recorded as an author decision and will keep the project `NOT_READY_FOR_SUBMISSION` until resolved.
