# Critical Reading Template

## Title

- Does the title accurately represent the study?
- Is the wording searchable and no stronger than the evidence?

## Abstract

- Are purpose, method, main result, uncertainty, and significance present?
- Can the abstract stand alone without unsupported claims?

## Background

- Are known, unknown, assumptions, and scope boundaries distinct?
- Does the introduction lead to a testable question and contribution?

## Data and methods

- Are variables, samples, windows, configurations, and methods sufficiently described?
- Are methods appropriate for the hypotheses and estimands?

## Results

- Does each result answer a stated question?
- Are alternative explanations, null results, and uncertainty represented?
- Do figures and tables support the written conclusions?

## Conclusion and future work

- Are conclusions calibrated and free of new evidence?
- Are limitations and next questions specific and testable?

## References

- Are citations relevant, verified, and used where the argument needs them?
