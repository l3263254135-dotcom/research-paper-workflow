# Figure Reader Path

Use the following default panel logic when it fits the evidence:

1. orient the reader to the object, design, or comparison;
2. show the primary observation or estimate;
3. show the relevant comparison, control, or uncertainty;
4. show robustness, boundary, or alternative explanation;
5. state the limited interpretation in the caption and adjacent prose.

The sequence is a design default, not a license to reorder evidence against the analysis plan. If the study requires another order, record the reason in `figure_storyboard.md`.
