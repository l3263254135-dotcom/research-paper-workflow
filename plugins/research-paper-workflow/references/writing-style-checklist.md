# Writing Style Checklist

- One main function per paragraph.
- Topic sentences make the paragraph's role visible.
- Old information connects naturally to new information.
- Subjects and verbs stay close enough to read easily.
- Active voice is used when it makes responsibility clearer; passive voice is retained when the process matters more than the actor.
- Tense is consistent with whether the sentence describes the study, a result, or established literature.
- Comparisons state both sides and use parallel forms.
- Modifiers sit next to the words they modify.
- Vague intensifiers and empty nouns are removed.
- Novelty, causality, mechanism, and certainty verbs match the evidence.
- Bias, ambiguity, unexplained abbreviations, and unnecessary jargon are removed.
