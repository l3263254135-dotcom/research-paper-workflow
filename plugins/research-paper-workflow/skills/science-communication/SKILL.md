# Science Communication

Use only after `claims.yaml` and `claim_calibration.md` are reviewed. The audience and format may change; the evidence boundary may not.

## Outputs

- conference abstract or extended abstract;
- oral presentation outline and slide plan;
- poster or graphical-summary plan;
- public or media explanation with a source note.

## Rules

- State the audience, purpose, time limit, and required format before drafting.
- Keep one narrative spine and one main message per slide or poster region.
- Prefer figures that can be understood without spoken explanation.
- Preserve uncertainty, limitations, and alternative explanations.
- Mark any simplification that removes technical detail.

## Acceptance

- Every public-facing conclusion maps to a calibrated claim.
- No headline, slide title, or poster label is stronger than the manuscript conclusion.
- The communication artifact is readable by the stated audience and fits the time or space limit.
