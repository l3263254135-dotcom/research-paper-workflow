# Cross Review

Review the manuscript and its evidence package as an independent process. Do not rely on the drafting agent's hidden context. Write `review_round_N.md`, `score_history.json`, `claim_calibration.md`, and `revisions_log_round_N.md`.

## Review lenses

Check significance and scope, experimental comparability, statistical object and uncertainty, mechanism versus association, figure support, citations, reproducibility, reporting requirements, and writing clarity.

Each finding must include severity, exact location, evidence status, smallest repair, fallback wording if unrepairable, and whether it blocks submission. Calibrate every claim as `strong`, `moderate`, `weak`, or `unsupported`; explain disagreements rather than averaging them away.

Do not reward cosmetic edits as scientific evidence. Preserve negative, null, reverse, and alternative explanations. Never convert the score into an official acceptance probability.
