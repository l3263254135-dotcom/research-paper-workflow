# Submission and Revision

Read the target journal's current official instructions and record URL, access date, and verification status. Keep author and institutional fields as explicit pending inputs when unavailable.

## Audit

Check article type, title page, abstract/word limit, key points, line numbers, references, figures, tables, SI, data/code/materials statements, funding, conflicts, authorship/CRediT, ORCID, AI disclosure, permissions, and license requirements.

For a revision, preserve the submitted version and maintain an opinion table with: reviewer comment, interpretation, action, evidence, manuscript location, response, and status. Verify every claimed change against the actual revised files.

Do not access a submission portal, submit, publish, or invent author, funding, ethics, repository, or policy information. Leave `NOT_READY_FOR_SUBMISSION` when a hard gate is unresolved.
