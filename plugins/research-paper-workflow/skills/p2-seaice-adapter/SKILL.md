# P2 Sea-Ice Adapter

Use with P2 files and read `references/p2-domain-rules.md`. Keep the scientific positioning at `fingerprint + process constraint` unless new evidence supports a stronger claim.

## Map

Track five nominal height cases, endpoint and adjacent contrasts, regional/seasonal sea-ice area and concentration, CICE area/volume/thickness, FLDS/Rnet, transport, storage, and budget accounts as separate variables with explicit masks and units.

## Hard boundaries

FLDS is not complete net surface heat flux; N_HEAT is not local ocean heat convergence; area, volume, and mass accounts are not additive; a height-shape feature is not a threshold; temporal precedence or co-location is not mediation; missing configuration, branch/restart, terrain, or land-surface evidence prevents a pure topographic attribution; an unclosed budget cannot be called a complete mechanism.

Report unavailable intermediate annual sequences, matched ocean storage, full fluxes, or monthly CICE as `BLOCKED` and complete independent annual/seasonal analyses.
